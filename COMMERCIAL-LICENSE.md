closed-source redistribution, or commercial hosting may contact the repository
owner, `wuxuyun0606-collab`, with:

- the legal name of the proposed licensee;
- the product, service, or deployment in which the project would be used;
- the intended distribution and hosting model;
- the requested term, territory, and number of users or deployments; and
- the Rainholm Fish version or commit to be licensed.

Any commercial rights require a separate written agreement signed by the
relevant parties. Terms may include fees, attribution, support, warranties,
indemnities, source-code access, and usage limits.

which it owns or controls the necessary rights. This commercial path does not
grant rights to `ai-fishing-game`, other upstream material, dependencies, or
third-party assets. Commercial use of the combined project may therefore also
require separate authorization from the upstream and other relevant rights
holders.
